Subclass 500
Student visa

Visit Australia to participate in a course of study.

Stay
Up to 6 years and in line with your enrolment.

Cost
From AUD2,500.00 (concessions apply in limited circumstances). A lower visa cost applies to eligible Pacific Island and Timor-Leste citizens, eligible ASEAN citizens and applicants in the ELICOS and Non-Award sector who lodge a valid Student visa application.

With this visa you can
- participate in an eligible course of study in Australia
- travel in and out of Australia
- work up to 48 hours a fortnight when your course of study or training is in session.

Check your eligibility
You must:
- apply online in or outside Australia
- be enrolled in a course of study in Australia and hold a valid Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) when the visa is decided
- hold Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC), or fall in one of the exemption categories
- be 6 years or older
- prove you have a welfare arrangement, if you are under 18
- if in Australia, hold an eligible substantive visa

Note:
- A CoE is no longer valid if it is cancelled or the course is complete
- A substantive visa ceases to be in effect if another substantive visa is granted
