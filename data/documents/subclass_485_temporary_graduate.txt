Document: Subclass 485 Temporary Graduate visa — Overview

Subclass 485
Temporary Graduate visa

A temporary visa that allows international students to live, study and work after you have finished your studies.

Basic Eligibility
You must:
- be aged 35 years or under when you apply (exceptions apply)
- hold an eligible visa
- have held a Student visa in the last 6 months
- have a recent qualification in a CRICOS-registered course
- apply for your eligible stream (it is not possible to change streams after you apply)
- attach required evidence when you apply

Post-Vocational Education Work stream
For international students who have recently graduated with an associate degree, diploma or trade qualification relevant to an occupation Australia needs.
- You can work in Australia (no hours restriction stated for this stream)
- You can bring your family with you
- Must hold a qualification relevant to the nominated occupation on the skilled occupation list
Stay: Up to 18 months. Hong Kong and British National Overseas passport holders may stay for 5 years.
Cost: From AUD5,750.00 (concessions apply in limited circumstances).

Post-Higher Education Work stream
For international students who have recently graduated with a degree from an Australian institution.
- You can work in Australia (no hours restriction stated for this stream)
- You can bring your family with you
- Need a recent degree in a CRICOS-registered course
Stay: Usually between 2 and 3 years, depending on qualification. Hong Kong and British National Overseas passport holders may stay for 5 years.
Cost: From AUD5,750.00 (concessions apply in limited circumstances).

Second Post-Higher Education Work stream
For Temporary Graduates holding a Post-Higher Education Work, Post-Study Work or Replacement visa with a degree from an Australian institution located in a regional area.
- You can work in Australia (no hours restriction stated for this stream)
- You can bring your family with you
Stay: Between 1 and 2 years depending on the regional location of the graduating institution and the regional area lived in while holding the visa.
Cost: From AUD2,265.00 (concessions apply in limited circumstances).

Note: Unlike the Subclass 500 Student visa (which caps work at 48 hours per fortnight while a course is in session), the Subclass 485 Temporary Graduate visa streams listed here do not carry a stated fortnightly work-hour cap — holders can work in Australia under their stream conditions. Always check the visa grant notice / VEVO for the specific conditions attached to an individual grant.
