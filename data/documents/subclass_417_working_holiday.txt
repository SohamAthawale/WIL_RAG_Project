Document: Subclass 417 Working Holiday visa — Overview

For young adults who want an extended holiday and to work here to fund it.

Basic Eligibility
- Must be 18 to 30 years old (or 35 years old for some countries)
- Must have a passport from an eligible country or jurisdiction
- Must not be accompanied by dependent children

First Working Holiday visa
- You can work while you are here
- You can apply for a second Working Holiday visa if you meet certain eligibility criteria
Stay: 12 months. Cost: AUD840.00.

Second Working Holiday visa
- For people who currently hold, or have held, a Working Holiday visa
- You can do any kind of work while you are here
- You must have completed 3 months of specified subclass 417 work
- If in Australia, must hold a substantive visa or last substantive visa expired less than 28 days ago
- You can apply for a third Working Holiday visa if you meet certain eligibility criteria
Stay: 12 months. Cost: AUD1,000.00.

Third Working Holiday visa
- For people who currently hold, or have held, a second Working Holiday visa
- You can do any kind of work while you are here
- You must have completed 6 months of specified subclass 417 work
- If in Australia, must hold a substantive visa or last substantive visa expired less than 28 days ago
Stay: 12 months. Cost: AUD1,000.00.

Note: All subclass 417 holders are also subject to visa condition 8547 — the 6-month work limitation with the same employer (see the WHM 6 month work limitation document) — unless an exemption applies. This is a categorically different work restriction from the Subclass 500 Student visa's 48-hours-per-fortnight cap; a 417 holder can work unlimited hours but must generally rotate employer/location every 6 months, whereas a 500 holder has no employer limit but is capped at 48 hours per fortnight while their course is in session.
