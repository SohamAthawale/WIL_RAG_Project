Document: Skills in Demand visa (Subclass 482) — Overview

This temporary visa lets an employer sponsor a suitably skilled worker to fill a position they can't find a suitably skilled Australian to fill. It is employer-sponsored, not a study-pathway visa.

Basic Eligibility
You must:
- be nominated for a skilled position by an approved sponsor
- have the right skills to do the job
- meet the relevant English language requirements

Core Skills stream
Employers address labour shortages by bringing in skilled workers where they can't source an appropriately skilled Australian worker. Occupation must be on the Core Skills Occupation List.
Stay: Up to 4 years (Hong Kong passport holders up to 5 years).
Cost: From AUD4,015.00 (concessions apply in limited circumstances).

Specialist Skills stream
Occupation must be an ANZSCO occupation in Major Groups 1, 2, 4, 5 or 6, with nominated salary meeting the Specialist Skills Income Threshold.
Stay: Up to 4 years (Hong Kong passport holders up to 5 years).
Cost: From AUD4,015.00 (concessions apply in limited circumstances).

Labour agreement stream
For skilled workers nominated by employers who have a Labour Agreement in effect with the Australian Government.
Stay: Up to a maximum of 4 years, depending on the terms of the labour agreement (Hong Kong passport holders up to 5 years).
Cost: From AUD4,015.00 (concessions apply in limited circumstances).

Subsequent entrant
For family members of subclass 457 and subclass 482 visa holders joining the primary visa holder in Australia, applying separately.
Stay: As long as the visa is valid.
Cost: From AUD4,015.00 (concessions apply in limited circumstances).

Note: Subclass 482 is employer-sponsored and tied to the nominated position — it is a fundamentally different category from the Subclass 500 Student visa (course-based, 48 hours/fortnight cap while course is in session) and the Subclass 485 Temporary Graduate visa (post-study work rights, no employer sponsorship required). Work rights under 482 are attached to the sponsoring employer and nominated occupation, not a fortnightly hour cap.
