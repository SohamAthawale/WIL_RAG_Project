Document: Working Holiday Maker (WHM) — 6 month work limitation (visa condition 8547)

Visa condition 8547 applies to anyone in Australia who holds any kind of Working Holiday Maker (WHM) visa — this includes Subclass 417 (Working Holiday) and Subclass 462 (Work and Holiday) — regardless of when they arrive. It also applies to anyone who holds a Bridging visa with condition 8547 imposed. It applies to any type of work (full time, part time, casual, shift or voluntary) unless an exemption applies.

Exemptions to condition 8547 (from 1 January 2024, you can work for the same employer for more than 6 months WITHOUT asking permission if you work in):
- different locations for the same employer, as long as work in any one location does not exceed 6 months (e.g. two hotels in the same chain, independently-owned franchises, different school/health facilities, self-employed providing services to the same business alongside other clients)
- plant and animal cultivation anywhere in Australia (e.g. harvesting/packing crops, pruning, maintaining animals for sale, processing animal/plant products)
- natural disaster recovery work anywhere in Australia
- critical sectors anywhere in Australia: agriculture, health, aged and disability care, childcare, tourism and hospitality
- certain industries in Northern Australia only: fishing and pearling, tree farming and felling, construction, mining

If work does not fall within an exemption, you can only work for the same employer for a maximum of 6 months while holding a WHM visa.

Requesting permission to work longer than 6 months
You may be given permission if you:
- have applied for a visa allowing ongoing full-time work and are awaiting the outcome, or
- undertake work critical to your employer, with a supporting letter attached to the request
Apply via the online form "Permission to work longer than 6 months". You can continue working while awaiting a written decision, provided the request was submitted before the 6-month period ended. If submitted after the 6 months ended, you must stop working for that employer until a decision is made.

Meaning of "same employer": the business or organisation you are directly working for. You cannot stay in the same position/location with the same employer for more than 6 months by rotating through different employment agencies, business affiliates, or sub-contracting arrangements. Workplace-based training counts as work.

Self-employment: your "employer" for condition 8547 purposes is the end user you provide services to. You must not provide services to the same end user for longer than 6 months unless an exemption applies or written approval has been obtained — unless that end user is not your only client.

Calculating the 6 months: starts from the day you start work (full-time, part-time, casual, shift or voluntary). It is based on elapsed months, not hours or days worked. The condition applies to each visa separately — the 6-month limit restarts when a new WHM visa is granted, or when a bridging visa with condition 8547 starts.

Note: this 6-month same-employer limitation is specific to Subclass 417 and 462 WHM visas. It does NOT apply to Subclass 500 (Student), 485 (Temporary Graduate) or 482 (Skills in Demand) visas, which have their own distinct work conditions.
